QuizVerse | Flutter Quiz & Learning App with Laravel Admin Panel

A self-hosted quiz, exam preparation, gamification, and learning platform for Android and iOS.

Overview

QuizVerse is a complete, self-hosted learning and quiz platform. It pairs a Flutter mobile app with a Laravel 12 REST API and browser-based administration panel. Create and manage quiz content, launch timed contests, run competitive battle modes, configure coins and VIP plans, and give learners multiple ways to practice.

What is included

  • Flutter mobile app for Android and iOS.
  • Laravel 12 backend REST API protected with Laravel Sanctum.
  • Web administration panel for content, settings, users, and monetization.
  • Database migrations and seeders for first-time setup.

Mobile app highlights

  • Quiz Zone with categories, levels, question play, results, and unlock flow.
  • Daily quizzes and login-streak rewards.
  • Live contests with contest details and leaderboards.
  • Battle Zone with one-vs-one and group battle flows, tiers, rankings, and bot support.
  • Exam preparation with categories, papers, instructions, timed play, results, and review.
  • Audio Quiz, Math Mania, Fun & Learn passages, and Guess the Word learning modes.
  • Leaderboards, profile, referrals, bookmarks, statistics, wallet history, coins, and VIP membership.
  • Email/password, mobile OTP, Google, Apple, and Firebase authentication flows.
  • Light/dark theme support, CMS pages, sharing, and audio playback.

Administration panel highlights

  • Dashboard and operational reporting.
  • Management for Quiz Zone, Audio Quiz, Daily Quiz, Math Mania, Battle Quiz, Exam Module, Fun & Learn, Guess the Word, and Contests.
  • Question creation, management, bulk operations, category/level assignment, and Quiz Zone import tools.
  • Content taxonomy, master question bank, level profiles, and moderation controls.
  • Coin packages, VIP subscriptions, advertising settings, transactions, and payouts.
  • Learner and staff management with role and permission controls.

Technology and requirements

  • Flutter / Dart mobile client.
  • Laravel 12 with PHP 8.2+ and MySQL 8.0+ recommended.
  • Laravel Sanctum authentication.
  • Composer, Node.js/npm, and a Flutter SDK compatible with Dart SDK constraint ^3.12.0.

Important

Demo URLs, credentials, store listings, payment products, Firebase configuration, Apple configuration, and production email settings are deployment-specific and must be configured by the buyer. Complete instructions are included in envato/index.html.
