Third-Party Services and Additional Costs

Please read before purchasing: QuizVerse is a self-hosted source-code package. Buyers must configure their own hosting, service accounts, and application credentials. Authentication and push notifications require a buyer-owned Firebase project; advertising and VIP subscriptions require additional setup when enabled. Third-party fees are separate from the item purchase price:

  • Firebase Authentication & Push Notifications: You must create and configure your own Firebase project for Email/Password, Phone OTP, Google Sign-In, Apple Sign-In, and Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM). Enable the required sign-in providers and configure platform credentials. Firebase offers no-cost allowances with service-specific limits; paid usage and billing requirements may apply, including SMS charges for Phone OTP. FCM itself is available at no cost. Review Firebase pricing and usage limits before deployment.
  • Google AdMob: To enable advertising, create your own AdMob account, register your Android and/or iOS apps, and configure your own AdMob app IDs and ad unit IDs. Your production advertising configuration must use your own account identifiers. AdMob account approval and applicable policies are managed by Google.
  • Google Play Developer Account: Your own account is required to publish on Google Play and offer Android VIP subscriptions through Google Play billing. Google charges a US$25 one-time registration fee. You must configure subscription products and the credentials required for purchase verification. See Google Play account requirements.
  • Apple Developer Program: Your own membership is required to publish on the App Store and offer iOS VIP subscriptions through Apple in-app purchases. Membership costs US$99 per year, or the applicable local price. Configure your app, subscription products, agreements, and purchase-verification credentials in your own Apple account. See Apple enrollment requirements.
  • VIP Subscription Setup: Subscription products must be created in the relevant store and mapped to your application configuration. Store review, payment agreements, taxes, and applicable store commissions are separate from this purchase. Developer membership does not guarantee app approval.
  • Web Hosting & Domain: A standard web server running PHP 8.3 or higher with MySQL 8.0 or higher is required to host the Laravel REST API and Admin Panel. Hosting and domain registration fees depend entirely on your chosen hosting provider (cPanel shared hosting, VPS, or cloud server).

Important Note: All third-party accounts, API credentials, domain names, SSL certificates, web hosting servers, and app store developer memberships must be created, owned, and managed directly by the buyer. No third-party subscription fees, hosting costs, or developer accounts are included with this purchase.

Provider pricing, eligibility, quotas, and policies may change. Check the linked provider pages for current terms before purchasing or launching your app.

Security

  • Laravel Sanctum bearer token authentication for API endpoints
  • Role-based access control (RBAC) for admin panel administrators
  • Parameterized SQL queries via Eloquent ORM to prevent SQL injection
  • Hardware-backed secure token storage (`flutter_secure_storage` Android Keystore & iOS Keychain) on mobile devices
  • Server-side receipt verification against Google Play Developer API and Apple App Store API
  • Web installer locks after completion — cannot be re-run from the browser
